Financial Statement Analysis (2025–2026)

Project Overview

This project examines and compares financial performance across two financial years, 2025 and 2026. The analysis focuses on profitability, liquidity, financial structure and cash flow to understand the company’s financial position and identify areas requiring management attention.

Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Microsoft Word

Financial Statements Analysed

* Income Statement
* Balance Sheet
* Cash Flow Statement

Key Areas of Analysis

* Revenue and profit growth
* Gross profit, operating profit and net profit margins
* Current ratio and working capital
* Total liabilities-to-equity ratio
* Interest coverage
* Operating cash flow and cash flow ratios

Key Findings

* Revenue increased by 25%, from ₦80 million to ₦100 million.
* Net profit increased by 29.41%, from ₦17 million to ₦22 million.
* Gross profit margin improved from 56.25% to 58%.
* Current ratio declined from 2.00x to 1.80x.
* Total liabilities-to-equity ratio increased from 1.00x to 1.09x.
* Operating cash flow increased by 38.89%.

Management Recommendations

* Monitor liquidity to ensure the business can meet its short-term obligations.
* Review liabilities and financing decisions to manage financial risk.
* Continue monitoring operating expenses.
* Strengthen cash collection and operating cash flow management.

Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how financial statements and financial ratios can be used to evaluate business performance, identify financial trends and support management decision-making.
