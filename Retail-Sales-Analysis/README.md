Retail Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using SQL and Power BI to identify top-performing products, compare regional performance, and support business decisions.

Tools Used

* MySQL Workbench
* Power BI
* DAX

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Revenue: ₦9.15 million
* Total Quantity Sold: 35 units
* Total Orders: 10
* Average Order Value: ₦915,000

Key Findings

* Phones generated the highest revenue at ₦3.75 million.
* Phones accounted for approximately 41% of total revenue.
* Tablets generated ₦1.8 million in revenue.
* Lagos recorded the highest regional revenue at ₦1.5 million.

Business Recommendations

* Maintain focus on strong-performing products while monitoring demand for other products.
* Investigate product and regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
* Consider product costs and profit margins before making decisions about product profitability.
